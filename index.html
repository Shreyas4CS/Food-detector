Two ways to learn

A recipe you can read, and a network that learns

Try one, check it on secret photos, then try the other way and compare.

Way 1

The colour recipe

The AI averages the numbers of your Fresh photos and your Spoiled photos. A new fruit goes to the recipe it is closest to.

# learned from your photos
Fresh = 3% brown, 78% colour
Spoiled = 31% brown, 52% colour
new fruit → closest recipe
Way 2

The neural network

A tiny computer brain. It looks at all 13 numbers together and finds its own way to sort them. It trains right here in the browser.

How it works

Teach an AI to tell fresh fruit from spoiled fruit, using your own photos. Each step unlocks the next.

  1. Your fruits and photosName your fruits and add at least 10 photos of fresh and spoiled fruit. Press Save to unlock the next step.
  2. Create a datasetCreate the two classes, then sort every photo into Fresh or Spoiled.
  3. Train your AIPick how it learns, see how the machine reads a photo, train and check it on secret test photos.
  4. TestingTest 5 or more new fruit, then finish the project.

What kind of AI is this?

  • Neural network: the app measures 13 things in every photo's pixels (brown patches, dark areas, spots, fuzz, texture and colours). A small neural network learns from those numbers.
  • Colour recipe: the AI works out the average look of each class from the same 13 numbers. A new photo joins the class it looks most like.
  • No fixed if/else rules and no made-up scores. Accuracy is shown only when there are enough test fruit.

Privacy and setup

  • Photos, labels and answers are saved only in this browser (local storage). No photo is sent to any server.
  • Open the file in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. The page works offline once loaded.
  • The camera needs an https:// page or localhost, and your permission. If it is blocked, use Upload Image.
  • Photos are resized to keep storage small. A browser can hold about 150 photos.
  • Reset deletes all photos, models and answers from this device.
